OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for Tank War 3D

Application: Tank War 3D • Package ID: com.hojiakbar.tankwar • Effective Date: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tank War 3D. This Privacy Policy informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our mobile application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Tank War 3D is designed as a standalone offline-first 3D military action simulation game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal identification information.

2. Information Collection and Use

Tank War 3D respects user privacy by applying data minimization:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise geographic location, photos, or files.
  • Local Game Progress Storage: All gameplay progress (such as unlocked tanks, credits, mission completions, medals, camouflage selections, and audio preferences) is stored strictly on your local device via Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • Hardware & Rendering Capabilities: To deliver 60 FPS 3D graphics, the app queries local device capabilities (such as OpenGL ES 2.0/3.0 support and screen resolution) purely in-memory during active gameplay. No hardware profiles are stored remotely.

3. Third-Party Services and In-App Updates

The application integrates standard, official Google Play libraries to ensure app stability and delivery of legitimate game updates:

  • Google Play In-App Updates: We use the Google Play App Update library to check if a newer version of Tank War 3D is available in Google Play Store. The update verification process is governed directly by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions and Device Access

Tank War 3D follows Google Play's least-privilege principle:

  • No access to camera, microphone, or external storage.
  • No background location access.
  • No dangerous runtime permissions are requested.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our service does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt corrective action.

6. Security

We value your trust. Because no sensitive personal data is transmitted over external servers, your gameplay experience remains private and contained within your Android environment.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
